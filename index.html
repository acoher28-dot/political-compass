    Result code and export

    Your result code summarizes your scores and how many statements you looked up. It has no name or personal information.

    What is sent: your scores, your topic scores, how many statements you marked "Don't know", your reading level, and your result code. This page does not collect a name, email, or device information.

    This is a rough self-reflection tool, not a validated psychological or political assessment. Answers can shift with mood, the news, or how a statement was read. Use the result as a starting point for thinking, not a verdict.
